About

Data science built on industry experience

I spent over 14 years in software testing at Sky, latterly as Test Manager, where I led cross-functional teams, optimised complex technical processes, and delivered a 25% reduction in resource demand through systematic quality engineering.

That experience taught me how large organisations actually work, where data gets lost, where decisions get made on instinct rather than evidence, and where machine learning can genuinely move the needle.

In 2025, I completed the Career Accelerator in Data Science with Machine Learning & AI (Certificate of Completion, University of Cambridge Institute of Continuing Education with FourthRev), formalising the analytical and engineering skills I now apply through RJ Data Voyage. My final employer project, a team prototype for a UK financial regulator, combined NLP sentiment scoring with financial-metrics extraction across public disclosures from systemically important banks.

I work with organisations that need practical, production-ready data solutions rather than slide decks and theory. If the problem involves unstructured text, complex classification, time-series forecasting, or turning messy data into clear strategic insight, I can help.

Expertise

Core competencies

Six areas where I deliver the most value, each backed by project work and production experience.

Machine Learning

Supervised and unsupervised methods including XGBoost, Random Forests, SVM, ensemble techniques, and clustering (K-Means, hierarchical). End-to-end pipeline development.

Natural Language Processing

Transformer-based models (FinBERT, BERT, GPT-2), topic modelling (BERTopic), sentiment analysis, text classification, and domain-specific NLP for financial and customer data.

Forecasting and Time Series

ARIMA/SARIMA, XGBoost, LSTM networks, hybrid models and decomposition techniques. Applied to demand planning and incident forecasting.

Data Visualisation and BI

Interactive dashboards and visual analytics using Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, and Power BI. Clear, decision-ready presentation of complex findings.

Statistical Analysis

Hypothesis testing (parametric and non-parametric), correlation analysis, regression, and model validation.

Anomaly Detection

Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM and statistical rules (IQR) for flagging irregular readings in operational sensor data, with results prioritised for expert review.
